SKYLIFT

Emergency help app for a client

My role
UI/UX Designer · 42 hours in Figma Make
Project
Emergency drone-dispatch iOS app with an interactive prototype
Goal
Make requesting aerial help a single tap, keep dispatch status always visible, and get critical medical info to responders fast
Delivered
Figma Make prototype, case study

I designed SKYLIFT, an emergency services app that helps someone who has been in an accident call a quadcopter for help. With a single tap, the app shares their location, sends the nearest SKYLIFT quadcopter to them, and gets them to a hospital connected to the SKYLIFT network quickly and efficiently.

How I approached it

Because people use this app under stress, I designed every screen around one clear action: a large help button in the centre of Home, three simple tabs, and live status and arrival time during dispatch.

Outcome

An interactive iOS prototype built to WCAG AA: all 36 colour pairs pass contrast in light and dark mode, and every control is at least 44px. Next, I'd test it with realistic emergency scenarios.

GENZ IN ACTION

Educational social media app for a client

My role
UI/UX Designer · 32 hours in Figma Make
Project
Educational social app for iOS, Android and desktop, including tablets
Goal
Help people share resources, learn and grow, and get educated in a friendly format
Delivered
Figma Make prototype, case study

I designed GENZ IN ACTION, an app all about collaboration and learning from each other in a social-media-like format. Its core goal is to help people share resources, learn and grow, and get educated in a friendly format.

How I approached it

I reframed the social feed as a learning loop: collaborate, share, learn and grow. Familiar patterns from apps Gen Z already use mean there's nothing new to learn, and clear privacy controls help young users feel safe.

Outcome

A clickable prototype for phones, tablets and desktop, with one bold brand identity carried into every screen. Next, I'd run usability tests with Gen Z users and design moderation tools for the community.

03 · Client

BYTEBALANCE

Nutrition tracker app for a client

My role
UI/UX Designer · 40 hours in Lovable
Project
Nutrition tracker app with a working prototype built in Lovable
Goal
Help people build and keep a healthy lifestyle by making it quick to log meals and water, see daily goals at a glance, and stay motivated
Delivered
Lovable prototype, case study

I designed BYTEBALANCE, a nutrition tracking app intentionally made to look familiar, like other calorie and fitness tracking apps. Its goal is to motivate users to stay healthy by tracking their calorie intake, so the habit forms naturally.

How I approached it

I kept the design deliberately familiar, with calorie rings, macro bars, streaks and a centre + button people already know from fitness apps, so new users need no explanation and logging a meal takes seconds.

Outcome

A working prototype built in Lovable in 40 hours, including Nutrition Buddy, an AI assistant for meal ideas and plans. Next, I'd time how quickly people log a meal and refine onboarding.

The short version

  • This site is designed to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA, which also covers India's accessibility standard, IS 17802.
  • It works with a keyboard, screen readers, browser zoom and your device's light or dark mode.
  • Some linked PDFs and Figma prototypes are hosted elsewhere and may not be fully accessible. I can send them in another format on request.
  • Need help or found a barrier? Email srv26uxd@gmail.com.

1. Commitment

As a UI/UX designer, I believe everyone should be able to use this portfolio, including people who use assistive technologies. Web accessibility means that people with disabilities can browse a site with the same ease and enjoyment as other visitors.

2. Conformance status

This website is designed to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, published by the W3C. WCAG 2.x Level AA is the benchmark used by accessibility laws and standards in many countries.

India: I am based in India, where the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 requires equal access to information and communication technology, and the Supreme Court has recognised digital access as part of the right to life under Article 21 (Amar Jain v. Union of India, 2025). India's ICT accessibility standard, IS 17802, is aligned with WCAG 2.1, so meeting WCAG 2.2 Level AA also covers it.

Status: partially conformant. The pages of this site are built to meet Level AA. Some third-party content linked from the site (see section 5) may not.

3. Accessibility features

  • Colour contrast: text meets at least 4.5:1 contrast, and large text and controls at least 3:1, in both light and dark mode.
  • Keyboard access: every link, button, menu and gallery can be used with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus outline.
  • Skip link: a "Skip to main content" link appears on the first press of Tab.
  • Structure: each page has one main heading, a logical heading order and labelled navigation, header, main and footer areas.
  • Page changes are announced: when you open a new page, focus moves to its heading and the browser tab title updates.
  • Images: every image has a text description. Decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Links: links that open a new tab or a PDF say so to screen reader users.
  • Touch targets: buttons and links are at least 44 by 44 pixels where possible.
  • Zoom and reflow: the layout adapts to 400% zoom and small screens without sideways scrolling.
  • Motion: animation is minimal and turns off when your device's "reduce motion" setting is on.
  • High contrast mode: controls keep visible borders in Windows high contrast mode.
  • No time limits, autoplay or flashing content.

4. Compatibility

This site is built to work with current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, and with screen readers such as VoiceOver (macOS and iOS), NVDA and JAWS (Windows), and TalkBack (Android). It is built with standard HTML, CSS, WAI-ARIA and JavaScript.

5. Known limitations

  • Linked PDFs: the MAKARANDAM and FAMGRAM case studies and handover documents are PDF files hosted by a third party and may not be fully tagged for screen readers. My resume and the SKYLIFT, GENZ IN ACTION and BYTEBALANCE case studies are hosted on this site as tagged PDFs.
  • Figma and Lovable prototypes and presentation: these open on Figma's and Lovable's websites, and their accessibility depends on those services.

If any of this content is hard to use, email me and I will send it in an accessible format, such as a plain-text or tagged document, or walk you through it.

6. How it was assessed

Accessibility was assessed by self-evaluation against the WCAG 2.2 AA success criteria during design and build, including colour-contrast checks, keyboard-only navigation and screen reader review.

7. Feedback and help

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or would like information in another format, contact me:

  • Email: srv26uxd@gmail.com

Please tell me the page, what you were trying to do, and the device or assistive technology you use. I aim to reply within 5 business days.
